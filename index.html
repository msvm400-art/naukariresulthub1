What Kind of Government Jobs Can I Get?

There are a wide variety of government jobs in India. It depends on your interests, but a job in the Indian government can range from policy-making, public administration, military, law, engineering, and even teaching.

  1. Indian Administrative Services (IAS) and Indian Foreign Services (IFS) Jobs: The role of an IAS officer may include advising the ministers on various policy issues, implementing policies of the government, collecting revenue, and supervising public spending. The starting monthly salary of an IAS or IFS officer is about INR 56,100.
  2. Indian Police Services (IPS) Jobs: A role with the IPS involves safeguarding the public, maintaining law and order, and detecting and preventing crime. According to Payscale, an entry-level police constable with less than 1-year experience can earn an average total compensation of INR 255,094.
  3. Banking Jobs: The banking sector is one of the fastest-growing sectors in India. Jobs in government-approved banks such as the State Bank of India (SBI), Allahabad Bank, HDFC, and ICICI are highly sought after. The starting monthly salary of a Probationary Officer (PO) is about INR 57,000 including allowances.
  4. Defence Jobs: Jobs with the National Defence Academy (NDA) can be in various departments, such as Army, Navy, Air Force, Coast Guard, etc. Cadet trainees receive a stipend of INR 56,100 per month.

Sarkari Result Exam for Government Jobs

Sarkari or Government exams are conducted for various positions in the state as well as central Government. There are various groups in Government jobs and candidates can apply for either of these groups, depending on their qualifications. Group A mostly comprises of managerial roles and are considered to be the highest level of jobs. Group B is for Gazetted officers. To clear the Group B exam, one has to make the UPSC exam. Most seats under Group B are filled via promotions, so only limited seats are left for entrance via exam. Group C and D are for public servants who have non-supervisory roles.

Q4. What is Sarkari Naukri and how is it different from private jobs?
Sarkari Naukri (government job) offers lifetime job security, a fixed salary with regular increments, pension benefits, medical facilities, housing allowances, and paid leave — benefits rarely found in private sector jobs. Government jobs also carry social prestige and authority. However, they require passing competitive exams, which millions of candidates appear for every year.
